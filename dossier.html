Cardiozep®

Generic Name: etavostatin sodium (Code: NVP-4821)

Medicine Summary

A specialized daily oral heart medicine in Phase III patient studies, developed for adult patients living with heart failure.

How Does it Work?

Cardiozep® combines two beneficial mechanisms: it helps the kidneys gently remove excess salt and fluid to reduce fluid build-up, while simultaneously relaxing blood vessels. This dual action helps relieve strain on the heart muscle so it can pump blood more easily throughout the body.

Clinical Patient Studies

The medicine is currently being tested in international Phase III clinical trials involving over 8,200 adult patients. The goal of the study is to evaluate symptom improvements, reduced hospital visits, and overall quality of life.

Manufacturing & Packaging

Cardiozep® is formulated as a convenient, film-coated oral tablet taken once daily. Manufactured in clean, modern pharmaceutical facilities adhering strictly to international Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) quality standards.

Quick Facts

  • Development Phase: Phase III (Patient Trials)
  • Target Condition: Heart Failure
  • How Taken: Once Daily Oral Tablet
  • Chemical Formula: C23H28N2O5S
  • Molecular Weight: 448.54 g/mol
