Medicine Safety & Side Effect Reporting

Patient safety is our top priority. Reporting any unexpected symptoms, side effects, or quality issues helps us ensure our medicines remain safe and effective for everyone.

Medical Emergency Notice
If you or someone else is experiencing an immediate life-threatening medical emergency or severe allergic reaction, please visit the nearest hospital emergency room right away or call emergency services.

How to Report a Side Effect

Patients, doctors, pharmacists, and family members are all welcome to report any side effect or product quality question. Our medical safety team reviews every submission promptly.

Four Simple Details Needed:

  • 1

    Patient Information

    Age, gender, or initials (your personal private details are kept confidential).

  • 2

    Medicine Name

    Name of the medicine, tablet strength (e.g., 5mg), and batch number if written on the box.

  • 3

    What Happened?

    A short description of the symptom or side effect, when it started, and how long it lasted.

  • 4

    Your Contact Details

    Your name and phone number or email so our medical team can reach you if needed.
